In Study ABC-101, mean age was 58.4 years in the drug X arm and 57.9 years in the placebo arm [CSR-ABC101, Table 14.1.1]. At week 12, drug X reduced seated systolic blood pressure by 11.8 mmHg compared with 4.1 mmHg on placebo (p < 0.001) [CSR-ABC101, Table 14.2.1]. Drug X lowered systolic blood pressure more than placebo at week 24 [CSR-ABC101, Table 14.2.1]. The treatment difference at week 12 was 7.9 mmHg [CSR-ABC101, Table 14.2.1]. Dizziness was reported by 9.0% of patients on drug X and 3.8% on placebo [CSR-ABC101, Table 14.3.1]. Serious adverse events were rare, occurring in 1.9% of drug X patients [CSR-ABC101, Table 14.3.2]. Headache occurred in 7.1% of patients on drug X [CSR-ABC101, Table 14.1.1].
